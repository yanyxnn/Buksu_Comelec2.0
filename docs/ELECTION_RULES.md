# ELECTION RULES

## General
Election behavior is configuration-driven. This document records agreed rules and points to `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` for unresolved institutional policy.

## Election-level abstention
A voter may have an election-level abstention option before entering contest voting if enabled by configuration. Complete abstention counts as participation because a valid submission was made.

## Contest-level abstention
Abstain is mutually exclusive with candidate selections in the same contest. One contest's abstention does not affect other contests.

## Voting methods
Current confirmed model uses single-choice and multi-choice candidate selections as configured. Voter-ranked-choice voting is not required; “rank” means result rank by final vote total.

## Year representatives
Representation groups are configurable by college + year. November currently targets 1st-Year Representatives per college. Future elections may configure other year groups.

## SBO
Current working rule: same-college voting/candidate pool, unless election configuration explicitly defines another scope.

## SSC
Current working rule: university-wide candidate pool for all eligible students, subject to final election configuration.

## Sectoral
Sector definitions and voter qualification remain an OPEN DECISION until officially confirmed.

## Ties
The system detects ties and flags the contest. The COMELEC President/authorized heads decide the institutional resolution outside the election engine. The system records the decision for audit/history.
